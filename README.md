## **Dataset of Interest:**  [US Truck-Involved Fatal Crashes](https://zenodo.org/records/20487070)
### This dataset contains fatal accidents involving at least one commercial truck for the years of 2018 to 2024 and is a subset of records collected by the the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Fatality Analysis Reporting System (NHTSA FARS). This data is exclusively for commercial transportation trucks and covers all 50 US states and Washington DC. The dataset itself contains relatively few fields but these fields contain extremely useful information namely the year, state, city, number of fatalities and most crucially the geographic coordinates of the accident. The inclusion of geographic coordinates opens up many possibilities for spatial analysis of this data in combination with other datasets. Some examples that come to mind would be to compare these accident locations to road quality or type of road as well as if accidents are occurring in settlements or on highway. With the right spatial data perhaps the impacts of rapid elevation changes or poor visibility due to sharp turns could be tested. Additionally analysis could be done at various administrative levels to see if certain policies enacted by the different states or cities correlate to higher or lower commercial trucking accident fatality rates. Unfortunately the data does NOT contain exact timestamps so trends related to the effects of weather or time of day would not be possible.
